    How it estimates.

    Every number comes from the listing in front of you and a set of market bands that ship with the extension.

    1. 1Reads the listing

      Title, company and location, taken from the job card on screen.

    2. 2Works out the level

      Seniority from the title, plus the employer’s tier and the local market.

    3. 3Applies a band

      Maps that combination to a market range in local currency, with the pay basis labelled.

    4. 4Shows its working

      A range, a signal strength, and the inputs it used, so you can judge it.

    Optional: Add your own Anthropic API key in settings to refine estimates for unusual titles. Without it, nothing leaves your browser.

    An estimate, and it says so.

    Ballpark gives you a reasonable starting point for a conversation about pay. It isn’t salary data, and it never shows what a specific person earns.

    Signal strength tells you how complete the listing was, not how accurate the number is. A strong signal on an unusual role can still be off.

    Installs in 60 seconds.

    Ballpark isn’t on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you load it yourself. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.

    Download ZIP
    1. 1Download

      Get the ZIP and unzip it anywhere you’ll keep it.

    2. 2Open extensions

      Go to chrome://extensions in a new tab.

    3. 3Developer mode

      Switch it on, top right of the page.

    4. 4Load unpacked

      Pick the unzipped folder, then open LinkedIn Jobs.
